I'm a PhD student working at the intersection of theoretical and observational cosmology. Advised by Josh Frieman and Scott Dodelson, I study evolving dark energy models and the growth of large-scale structure using the cosmic microwave background, galaxy surveys, and supernovae. I am also interested in the statistical properties of the anomalies in the CMB and their implications for cosmology.

Research

  • Growth of structure versus geometry: the CMB and DES Y6
