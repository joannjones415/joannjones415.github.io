Constraints on a broad class of physically motivated dynamical dark energy models

Most evidence for evolving dark energy comes from fits to the w0wa parameterization, which is hard to connect to physics. I constrain the simplest physically motivated models of dynamical dark energy, in which a slowly rolling scalar field drives cosmic acceleration, with DES Y6 3×2pt, DESI DR2 BAO, the DES-Dovekie supernovae, and CMB data from Planck, ACT, and SPT. A modified CAMB integrates the model's background and perturbations directly.

Advised by J. Frieman, with A. Shajib · UChicago · 2024–present

The S8 tension through a different lens

The CMB measures clustering at two epochs: at recombination through the primary anisotropies, and near z ≈ 2 through lensing. A single CMB value of S8 mixes the two. I marginalize over lensing with a few principal components of the lensing power spectrum, so that Planck, ACT, and SPT give S8 from recombination alone, and I compare it with DES Y6 and with the CMB lensing reconstruction.

Advised by S. Dodelson, with U. de Andrade Uchoa · UChicago · 2025–present

Uncorrelated anomalies in the CMB

The CMB shows several anomalies on large angular scales. Using simulated ΛCDM skies, I showed that they are close to uncorrelated with one another, so their probabilities multiply. Together they give a p-value of about 3 × 10−8, more than a 5σ departure from ΛCDM.

Advised by G. D. Starkman and C. J. Copi, with Y. Akrami · CWRU · 2023–present

Previous projects

Machine learning for relativistic electron precipitation

I developed an unsupervised machine learning method to find relativistic electron precipitation bands in years of SAMPEX electron count data. It combines power spectral density with a self-organizing map, a competitive neural network, to catalog the band events automatically.

Advised by S. Vidal-Luengo and L. Blum · LASP, CU Boulder · summer 2022

  • talkCUWiP, Brown University, January 2023

Macroscopic dark matter search

I tested machine learning methods for image classification in a citizen science project that connects the search for macroscopic dark matter with public outreach.

cmb-lensing-visualizer

Deform the CMB lensing potential by an amplitude AL, or by the first two lensing principal components, independently of the cosmology that generated it, and watch the lensed TT spectrum and its residuals respond.

Runs in the browser · source on GitHub

cmb-anomaly-correlations

Computes four large-angle anomaly statistics on the Planck 2018 temperature maps and on ΛCDM realizations, and turns them into individual and joint p-values. It reproduces the analysis in arXiv:2310.12859.
